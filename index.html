FAQ

What is DTT: Defend The Tree?

DTT: Defend The Tree is a defense race mobile game made by LOEND, an indie game studio in Jeju, Korea. It combines tower defense with time attack: every player gets the same stage, and the fastest clear wins.

Is DTT a good tower defense game to try?

If you like tower defense games but want faster pace, DTT is built for you. You don't need a 2× speed button: you throw skill cards at the right moment, build your party with guardians, runes, augments and relics, and race the clock.

Is DTT an indie game?

Yes. DTT is an indie game developed by LOEND, a small five-person team.

Is DTT a new game?

Yes. DTT launched in Early Access on the App Store in September 2026, and Android pre-registration is open on Google Play.

Does DTT have PvP?

Yes. In ranked play you clear the same stage as other players and compete on clear time. The fastest records climb the ranking board, so there is no real-time matchmaking or waiting.

Does DTT have PvE or a single-player mode?

Yes. Story mode lets you fight through chapters of enemy waves with a boss at the end of each chapter, and it can be played offline.

Is DTT free to play?

Yes. DTT is free to download with optional in-app purchases. Drop rates for probability-based items are published on this site.

Which platforms is DTT on?

iOS (App Store, Early Access) and Android (Google Play, pre-registration).

Who is DTT for?

Players who enjoy tower defense or survival games, want strategy without tricky dodging controls, like short sessions and ranking races, or love cute animal characters.
