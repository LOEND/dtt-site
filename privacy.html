Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

LOEND ("we", "us") operates the mobile game DTT: Defend The Tree (the "App"). This policy explains what information the App handles and how it is used.

1. Information we collect

The App does not require account registration; guest play is fully supported. The App may collect:

  • Device and identifiers — device model, OS version, language, app version, advertising identifier (IDFA/GAID), and an app-instance identifier.
  • Gameplay data — progress, scores, clear times, rankings, and in-game settings.
  • Diagnostics — crash reports and performance logs.
  • Usage analytics — screens viewed, sessions, and in-app events.

We do not collect your name, email address, precise location, or contacts unless you send them to us directly (for example, when you contact support).

2. Third-party services

  • Google AdMob — displays ads. May use the advertising identifier and usage data to serve and measure ads. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Authentication) — usage analytics, crash reporting, and anonymous guest sign-in. Firebase Privacy
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — process in-app purchases. We never receive your payment card details.

3. How we use information

  • To operate the App, save progress, and run rankings
  • To fix crashes and improve stability and balance
  • To show ads and measure their performance
  • To respond to support requests

4. Advertising choices

You can limit ad personalization on your device: iOS — Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, or Apple Advertising; Android — Settings › Google › Ads.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with the service providers listed above as needed to operate the App, or when required by law.

6. Children

The App is rated 13+ and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Data retention and deletion

Gameplay data is retained while the App is in service. You may request deletion of your data at any time by emailing support@loendgame.com with your in-game player ID. We will process the request within 30 days.

8. International transfer

Data may be processed on servers operated by our service providers outside your country, including the Republic of Korea and the United States.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
