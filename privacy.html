Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 20, 2026 · Last updated: September 9, 2026

Loend ("the Company", "we", "us", or "our") complies with applicable personal information protection laws and regulations, and establishes and discloses this Privacy Policy to protect users' personal information and handle grievances related thereto promptly and smoothly. This Policy applies to the mobile game service "DTT: Defend The Tree" (iOS / Android).

Article 1 (Personal Information Collected)

The Company collects the following personal information for user registration, service provision, customer support, and related operations:

  1. Information Collected During Sign-Up and Login
    • Guest Login: Device identifier (advertising identifier, IDFA/GAID), Firebase Anonymous Authentication UID
    • Google Login: Google account identifier, email address, nickname (and profile image if selected)
    • Apple Login: Apple account identifier, email address (if user chooses to share)
  2. Information Automatically Collected During Service Use
    • Device Information: OS version, device model name, device identifier, language and country settings
    • Connection Information: IP address, access date and time, access logs
    • Game Play History: Play data, progress status, scores, in-game activity logs
    • Payment Information: Transaction date and time, payment amount, product name, transaction ID (financial details such as credit card numbers are not collected)
    • Advertising Identifier (IDFA/GAID): For serving advertisements and measuring ad performance
    • Fraudulent / Unauthorized Use Records
  3. Information Collected During Customer Support Inquiries
    • Email address, inquiry details, account identifier

Article 2 (Purpose of Collection and Use of Personal Information)

The Company uses collected personal information for the following purposes:

  • Member identification and identity verification
  • Service provision, operation, content delivery, and game data synchronization
  • Fee payment, settlement, and refund processing related to the use of paid services
  • Responding to customer inquiries and delivering important service notices
  • Preventing unauthorized/fraudulent use and taking disciplinary measures against violators of the Terms of Service
  • Service improvement, statistical analysis, and service optimization
  • Delivering personalized advertising and measuring advertising effectiveness

Article 3 (Retention and Use Period of Personal Information)

The Company retains personal information during the period of service provision. Upon account deletion/withdrawal, personal information is retained for thirty (30) days and then destroyed without delay. However, if retention is required under applicable laws and regulations, the Company retains the relevant information for the designated statutory periods:

  • Records on contracts or withdrawal of offers/cancellations: 5 years (Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce)
  • Records on payment and supply of goods/services: 5 years (Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce)
  • Records on consumer complaints or dispute resolution: 3 years (Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce)
  • Records on labeling/advertising: 6 months (Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce)
  • Service access log records: 3 months (Protection of Communications Secrets Act)
  • Records of fraudulent / unauthorized use: 1 year (Internal policy)

Article 4 (Provision of Personal Information to Third Parties)

In principle, the Company does not provide users' personal information to external third parties, except in the following cases:

  • When the user has given prior consent
  • When required by laws and regulations, or upon lawful requests by investigative authorities in accordance with procedures and methods prescribed by law

Article 5 (Consignment / Outsourcing of Personal Information Processing)

To ensure seamless service provision, the Company entrusts personal information processing operations to third-party service providers as follows:

  1. Google LLC (Firebase, Google Play, AdMob)
    • Firebase Authentication: Member authentication and login processing
    • Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics: Service analytics and crash/error reporting
    • Firebase Cloud Messaging: Push notification delivery
    • Cloud Firestore / Realtime Database: Game data storage and management
    • Google Play Billing: In-app purchase and billing processing
    • AdMob: Advertisement serving and advertising identifier processing
  2. Apple Inc.
    • Apple ID authentication processing (when using Sign in with Apple)
    • App Store: In-app purchase and billing processing

Article 6 (Rights of Users and Legal Guardians and Methods of Exercise)

  • Users or their legal guardians may at any time request to inspect, correct, delete, suspend processing of, or withdraw consent for personal information concerning themselves or children under the age of 14.
  • Such requests may be submitted via email to support@loendgame.com, and the Company will process them without undue delay.
  • If a user requests the correction of inaccurate personal information, the Company will not use or provide such information until the correction has been completed.

Article 7 (Procedures and Methods of Personal Information Destruction)

  1. Destruction Procedures: Once the purpose of collection has been achieved, personal information will be stored for thirty (30) days following account withdrawal and then permanently destroyed. Information required to be preserved under relevant laws will be transferred to a separate database (DB) and securely preserved.
  2. Destruction Methods
    • Electronic file format: Permanently deleted using technical methods that render the records irrecoverable
    • Paper documents: Shredded with a paper shredder or incinerated

Article 8 (Protection of Personal Information of Children Under 14)

  • When collecting personal information of children under the age of 14, the Company obtains the consent of their legal guardian.
  • Legal guardians may request to inspect, correct, delete, or suspend the processing of the child's personal information.

Article 9 (Security Measures for Personal Information Protection)

The Company takes the following technical, administrative, and physical measures to ensure the security of personal information:

  • Administrative Measures: Establishment and implementation of internal management plans, regular internal security checks
  • Technical Measures: Utilization of Firebase security controls, encryption during data transmission (HTTPS/TLS), access authority management
  • Physical Measures: Storing personal data within the secure infrastructure facilities of Google Cloud Platform

Article 10 (Advertising Identifiers and Tracking)

The Company utilizes advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID) to serve ads and evaluate ad effectiveness through Google AdMob. Users can opt out of or reset their advertising identifier through device settings:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking / Apple Advertising
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset / Delete advertising ID
